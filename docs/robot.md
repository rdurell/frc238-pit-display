<section class="pit-panel" markdown="1">
## Built by Team 238

FIREFLY is designed, built, and programmed by the students and mentors of
*FIRST*<sup>&reg;</sup> Robotics Team 238. Every part of the robot is an
opportunity to practice engineering, teamwork, and problem solving.

Competition robots are named after DC Comics villains. The practice robot
uses the villain's real name, keeping the team's naming tradition alive.
</section>
